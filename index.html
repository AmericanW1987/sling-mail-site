Sling-Mail

An email app for Windows and Android that keeps your mail on your own device.

  • Works with Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft 365 and any email account that supports IMAP and SMTP.
  • Sign in with Google or Microsoft on their own sign-in pages. Sling-Mail never sees those passwords.
  • Your messages, sign-ins and settings are stored only on your computer or phone. Sling-Mail has no servers of its own.
  • A calendar that works with Google Calendar, and a home dashboard with National Weather Service forecasts, a to-do list and notes.

Sling-Mail is a small independent app, shared with family, friends and colleagues.
